What I'm engaged for

My clients are pharmaceutical, medtech and manufacturing companies running programs where process, quality and systems change together. I work as interim project or program manager, or as the PMO lead behind one.

You need one global process across every site and business unit.

I lead standardization programs end to end: the current state, the target process, the level of standardization each unit will accept, and the governance that holds it in place. Quality, operations and IT work as one project team, not three.

Done before: project lead for the global standardization of change control across all Boehringer Ingelheim business areas, and for BISQUIT, the group-wide alignment of supplier qualification.

Your rollout has to land in dozens of countries at once.

Design is half the job; adoption is the other half. I plan deployment waves, train local key users and track readiness site by site until the new process is used, not just installed.

Done before: led GOTrack, the deployment and training program for quality business processes across 60 countries, more than 90 sites and about 700 end users at Boehringer Ingelheim.

Your SAP program needs someone fluent in both the business and the system.

I started as an SAP consultant at SAP AG in 1996 and have taken production, maintenance and quality modules through full implementation lifecycles. I translate process requirements into system design and keep data migration, testing and training on plan.

Done before: led the SAP EMEA implementation for production, maintenance and quality within Carlsberg's 17-country standardization program; module lead at Feldschlösschen; global SAP procure-to-pay support at Bayer.

Your project needs structure without slowing down.

I run projects to PMI standards and use Scrum and Kanban where they fit. Steering committees get clear, regular reporting: status, risks and the decisions needed from them.

Done before: regular reporting to senior leadership and the Board of Directors at Boehringer Ingelheim; certified Scrum Master, Product Owner and Boehringer Ingelheim Agile Facilitator for business design.
