What I'm engaged for

My clients are pharmaceutical, medtech and manufacturing companies running programs where process, quality and systems change together. I work as interim project or program manager, as the PMO lead behind one, or as coach to the leaders and teams going through the change.

You need one global process across every site and business unit.

I lead standardization programs end to end: the current state, the target process, the level of standardization each unit will accept, and the governance that holds it in place. Quality, regulatory, procurement and IT work as one project team, not four.

Done before: led the global harmonization of change control at Boehringer Ingelheim for 80 sites and 4,600+ users, live in November 2020, then its extension to end-to-end regulatory change control for 2,500 users, live in April 2023. Project lead for BISQUIT, the global standardization of supplier qualification across 21 country and market groupings, live in December 2018.

Your rollout has to land everywhere at once.

Design is half the job; adoption is the other half. I plan deployment, build key-user networks, run testing and training at scale and track readiness site by site, until the new process is used, not just installed.

Done before: led the global Deviation Management 2.0 transformation at Boehringer Ingelheim: a big-bang go-live for about 13,000 users in September 2023, with 218 key users, more than 120 user-acceptance testers, training in six languages and 23 data models covering about 100,000 records. On change control, more than 62,000 end-user training hours.

Your new system has to fit the way the business works.

Quality systems fail when they are configured around the software instead of the process. I translate process requirements into system design and keep integration, data, testing and validation on plan, from the first workshop to go-live.

Done before: eight years of global TrackWise rollouts at Boehringer Ingelheim, from complaint management in 2015 to Deviation Management 2.0 in 2023, including integration with regulatory and master-data systems. Earlier, SAP plant maintenance stream lead for the Carlsberg group template across 17 countries, and SAP consultant at SAP AG.

Your project needs structure without slowing down.

I run projects to PMI standards and deliver in sprints where that fits. Steering committees get clear, regular reporting: status, risks and the decisions needed from them.

Done before: sprint-based delivery on each Boehringer Ingelheim program, from 8 sprints for change control to 12 for its regulatory extension; regular reporting to senior leadership and the Board of Directors. PMP since 2012; Professional Scrum Master and Product Owner.

Your leaders and teams need a coach, not just a plan.

Systems go live on a date; people change over months. As a certified systemic coach I work one to one with leaders and project managers, coach teams through reorganization and transformation, and facilitate the workshops where decisions get made. Coaching is the part of my work I have trained in most.

Done before: a three-year leadership development program for the LKH Wolfsberg hospital; individual leadership coaching since 2002; a reorganization and efficiency program for Kostmann; the human resources policy adopted by all four KABEG hospitals.
